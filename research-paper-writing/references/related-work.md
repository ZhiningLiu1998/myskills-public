# Related Work

Related Work helps a reviewer see where the paper sits and what is new about
it. It should make the difference from the closest work easy to check.

## Organize by topic

Group papers by technical approach, usually in two to four topics: mainstream
methods for the task, methods closest to the core idea, and techniques the
method builds on. Ordering by year alone hides the relationships.

Start the list with the strongest and most recent competitors. Leaving out a
close baseline is one of the most common review objections.

## Paragraph pattern

For each topic:

1. Say what the topic covers.
2. Summarize the shared approach of the representative methods.
3. State the limitation that matters for this paper's challenge.
4. Say how this paper differs.

Compare mechanisms, assumptions, and failure modes, not adjectives. A list of
citations with one clause each does not tell the reader anything.

## Check

- Are the closest and strongest prior methods discussed?
- Is the difference stated in technical terms?
- Are the claims about other papers accurate and cited?
